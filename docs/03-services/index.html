What this is: the at-a-glance index of every third-party service Forge touches — what triggers adopting it, what it costs, how hard it is to leave, and whether it's actually set up yet. Start here before opening any individual runbook.

Total cost

Every adopted service costs $0/month today. The only committed spend anywhere in the plan is $25 one-time (Google Play, at T1) and $99/year (Apple Developer, at T2) — and the Apple charge doesn't start until its trigger fires.

Needs William's eye

Sentry is PROPOSED, not decided. It's a genuine coin-flip against Firebase Crashlytics: Crashlytics is free, unlimited, and already inside the Firebase stack, but its Flutter web support is poor — and web is the beta channel, which is why Sentry is the current default. Flip it back to Crashlytics if the beta ends up mobile-only.
