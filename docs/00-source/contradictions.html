Needs your input

Only one conflict genuinely needs you: ruling #1, the scope of the business.

Your earliest notes describe "AlpFit" as three products — a consumer app, gym management software, and trainer software. Every formal document after that describes one consumer app in which "gym" is just an account type. The plan proceeds on one app, because that is the unmistakable direction of travel. If you still intend the three-product business, say so and the roadmap changes shape significantly.

Rulings #7 (feed widget grid) and #9 (groups) are minor and deferred rather than decided. Glance at them if you disagree that they can wait.

Direction of travel

Four places where the notes show your thinking moving in a clear direction over time. These patterns drove several rulings above.

Ambition narrowed

The three-platform AlpFit vision never resurfaces after the first brainstorm. Every formal deliverable scopes down to one app with gym accounts inside it.

Dashboard became Feed

An early split between a personal "Dashboard" and a separate "Social" page collapsed into one Feed that does both jobs.

Goals got promoted

Filed as a stretch item, then given more design attention than anything else in the corpus, then fully built. Effort revealed the real priority.

Calendar views never stabilized

Three documents give three different lists of view granularities, each dropping or renaming an option. The code broke the tie.

Correction to the intake pass

An automated intake pass reported that Bigger Rec Manager Meeting.docx was a duplicate of the gsutil CORS command note, and that the real meeting notes did not exist. That was wrong, and it was caught by re-reading the file directly.

The file contains genuine stakeholder notes: what you need from a stakeholder (feedback on design and UI, feedback on features implemented, and a beta group), and the split of that beta group into "The Gym" — who would host their own community and use it to communicate news and events — and "The User" — people who use the app and participate in the host gym's community.

This matters because it is the only record of the beta arrangement anywhere in your notes, and Milestone 3 is built directly on it.
