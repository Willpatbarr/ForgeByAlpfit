App Overview:
	Purpose: Connect people through fitness
	How will it do this?: 
	•	Allow new attendees to meet and learn about their gym through online channels
	•	Allow current attendees to meet and communicate with others through online channels
	•	Allow a gym to communicate with their customers through said channels
	•	Allow users to plan workouts together

What do I need from a stakeholder?:
	•	Feedback on design decisions (styling and UI elements)
	•	Feedback on features implemented
	•	A group to beta test both parts;
	•	The Gym: would host their own community and use it to communicate news and events. As well as provide a place for people to connect
	•	The User: people who would use the app and participate in the host gym’s community
	•	Feedback from beta testers on usage experience
