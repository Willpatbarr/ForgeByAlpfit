Mission Statement:
To create a one-stop-shop for people to connect with their gym, trainer, and other gym goers in order to create a more inviting and connected experience for gym communities.

Plans: 
Create multiple platforms that can all be used independently or together:
	•	Consumer level mobile app:
	•	Plan workouts
	•	Share and add people to workouts
	•	Social features
	•	Connect and communicate with your trainer (if applicable)
	•	Connect with your gym for enhanced social features
	•	Gym software:
	•	Website and mobile application to be able to manage members
	•	Manage a social media-esque presence directly connected to your gym
	•	Trainer software:
	•	Website and mobile app
	•	Plan workouts for clients
	•	Communicate with clients

Forge Clientside goals: -
	•	Ability to look at and join a gym’s community —> mapping system or a list at first
	•	Ability to create and schedule a workout —> workout planner, manual exercise adding at first
	•	Viewable calendar with your’s and other’s lifts —> calendar page
	•	Manage a friend’s list —> friends list page
	•	Join, invite or request either of friends with workouts —> get to from calendar, friends profile, or your workouts list
	•	Create shareable goals with friends —> create page, look at friends goals from their profile, look at yours on dashboard
	•	Post to communities. —> communities page

Pages for Forge:
	•	Dashboard —> display goals and workouts
	•	Calendar —> displays events at different sizes
	•	Create —> create workouts or goals
	•	Social —> social hub with split off pages for friends and communities
	•	settings/profile —> edit friends/communities/profile settings, preview public profile

Dashboard page:
	•	Display your workout for the day
	•	Display any of your pinned goals
	•	Display any goals your friends have shared with you/ you’re an accountability partner for
Calendar page:
	•	Display a calendar in either a month, week, or day format
	•	Show public events from your communities and friends
	•	Show your public and private events
	•	Allow you to edit and add events onto the page
	•	Adding an event prompts you for a new event or add existing event

Create page:
	•	View created events to edit or add to calendar
	•	Allows you to create events of three types: workout, goal, and basic event
	•	Creating one of these will give you the option to make it public or private, add it to calendar, or share with friends

Social page:
	•	View a feed of updates from your friends and communities
	•	Be able to search for friends and communities
	•	Be able to view friends and communities
	•	When viewing communities be able to see their feed

Settings/Profile page:
	•	Adjust user settings and themes
	•	View the public appearance of your pofile
