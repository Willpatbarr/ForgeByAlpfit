Project Purpose
There is a barrier of intimidation and self-consciousness that keeps people from starting a fitness journey. Many times I have heard from peers that they would love to start working out and exercising, but they are scared of looking dumb or that they don’t know what to do. These same people, if they eventually attend a gym consistently, admit that they should’ve never worried in the first place. Almost every person in a gym is more than willing to get to know and help anyone else.
Background/Prior Knowledge
My personal relation to this is fairly extensive. I have been working out consistently for about 3 years now. During that time I have helped a lot of peers through planning and starting workouts so they feel more comfortable doing so themselves. 
I have also taken the class on campus ESS 381, Strength Training Theory and Application. The purpose of this class was to teach someone how to be a personal trainer. We learned about basic meal planning and nutrition, how to write a workout program for various populations and many time frames, and how to properly teach someone how to execute certain exercises.
I wouldn’t consider myself to be an expert by any means, but I do believe I have a fairly comprehensive understanding of exercise and training.
As far as I can find this specific type of app has not been created. There are many apps that focus on delivering high quality programming or meal planning tools. This app would not try to enter that market, but merely touch on those. The main focus would be on the social features of the app.
I have been planning this app for about two years now. Many iterations of features and requirements have been thought up, but the goal has always been the same. I have not done much coding on the app as I have been waiting for my Senior Project to do it.
That ESS 381 class I took to help me understand the needs of clients and personal trainers better. I shadowed the trainers at the gym and talked with them about this app and what they would like to see in it as a trainer, this feedback has been worked into the requirements and features I plan to implement.
Description
Provide the details of your project. In particular, make sure to include:
This app will be a place people can go to to plan and schedule workouts with their friends, or new friends they can meet through the community of their gym. In that same community gyms could post updates or news. New attendees would also be able to go and join a gym’s community before even attending the gym, getting to know people and asking questions so they feel more prepared for their first workout.
Here are the current major features I have planned:
	•	Users can create workouts
	•	Friends can be added to workouts and scheduled on an in app calendar
	•	Workouts can be connected to specific gyms
	•	Users can add other users to their friends list
	•	Users can join communities hosted by a gym
	•	Gyms can host a community that will allow them to create and manage different channels for updates and posts.
	•	Users can interact with and post on certain channels of a gym’s community
I have not seen an app dedicated to this. I have seen solutions like facebook pages/groups for gyms or other social channels created, but nothing made for this from the start.
I hope the real world impact of this app would be more people feeling comfortable starting to exercise consistently and more well connected gym communities. Communities are important for us as humans and I think in a place where people wear headphones the whole time they attend, there can be a bit of separation between everyone.
The intended audience would be adults ranging from 18-late 50s really. The majority of users would probably be younger adults who use other social media. Where the primary goal is to help people feel more comfortable attending a gym, I want the app to be usable by all ages. Almost all users would fall into one of three categories; a new gym attendee looking for help, a consistent attendee looking to connect with other attendees, and a gym that would like to host its own community. 
I will know the project is done when the above requirements have been met to the satisfaction of myself and the stakeholders. I think at that point the product would be valid and demo-able to portray the full scope of the app’s goals.
Significance
My project will be significant because of the media aspect of it. It’s not hard to create a workout planning app. The majority of my learning and time will go into learning how to make an app that multiple users can interact through.
I think this would be a very applicable resume project. I will have to have multiple frontend clients as well as some kind of cloud service to allow communication.
New Computer Science Concepts
I will need to learn more about mobile app/web design for the frontend clients. In order to allow users to interact I will also have to learn how to use and integrate a cloud service. User account handling practices will be new to me as well as creating and using large databases that integrate into a frontend client.
Interestingness
I am excited about this because I have had the idea for this app since my mission 3 years ago. I love helping people feel accomplished and energized through achieving fitness goals and I believe this app will do that. I also think that a strong sense of community will always benefit someone and with gym attendance becoming more and more popular there is an even bigger opportunity for building that sense of community.
