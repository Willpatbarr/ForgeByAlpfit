sdWhat do I want the app to be able to do?:
	•	Users can create workouts
	•	Users can add friends to that workout and schedule it for a specific time
	•	Users can join gyms and participate in that community
	•	Gyms can post news and events
	•	Users can schedule workouts for specific gyms

Features to Include
	•	Insights on when you’ve done a given movement better and what has impacted it (e.g. to a certain song, certain time, or before or after another movement)
	•	Share workouts with other people
	•	Have scheduled workouts that you can add your buddies to. Option to remind you of the workout and confirm you’re going, notifies you if your buddy cancels or not.
	•	Track maxes on each movement
	•	Option to use notifications to update the app (to avoid having to reopen the app each time)
	•	Link programs to specific places. This would allow you to choose which workouts you wanna do based on the gym that you are going to. Your gym buddies can then share the workouts they have for different areas. Eventually change this so that different gyms will have the most popular workouts that can be seen from a map view similar to pokemon go.
	•	

Aspects to be completed
	•	Database of movements
	•	Full UI
	•	Draft UI
	•	Color palette
	•	pages/menus

Here are the current major features I have planned:
	•	Users can create workouts
	•	Friends can be added to workouts and scheduled on an in app calendar
	•	Workouts can be connected to specific gyms
	•	Users can add other users to their friends list
	•	Users can join communities hosted by a gym
	•	Gyms can host a community that will allow them to create and manage different channels for updates and posts.
	•	Users can interact with and post on certain channels of a gym’s community
